**Diagnosis (not a reason to proceed):**
- The only difference is in NVS: the `cal_data` entry of the `phy` namespace (RF PHY calibration) was re-written, together with its blob chunks.
- The `nimble_bond` entry and the BLE security keys were not changed.
- This matches ESP-IDF behaviour: the legacy v7.12.0 firmware updates this entry when the radio (BLE) is initialised at boot.
- The device has been reset/booted several times since the A2 dump (serial capture, `flash-id --after hard-reset`).
- Firmware, bootloader, partitions and filesystem are byte-identical to the backup.

Under the mission's fail-closed rule, the A3B was stopped here.

## Second blocker found in the committed candidate firmware (c1fb3dfd)

- `firmware/LEX_MACHINA_DEVICE_V1_CANDIDATE/lex_device_v1.h:211` accepts only `#LEXMACHINA|DEVICE_VERSION|2`.
- The `LEXV1.VER` generated by the builder and present on the SD is `DEVICE_VERSION|3`.
- Flashed unchanged, the firmware would print "LEXV1.VER ausente/invalido" and turn off all DEVICE V1 layers (a valid runtime refused by mistake). That is a rollback trigger.
- `DEVICE_DATA_CONTRACT_V1.md:130` also still says `DEVICE_VERSION 2`.
- In addition, the boot diagnostic in the candidate does not cover everything the A3B asks for:
  - queries 22.XXIX, 24 §4 and 114.VIII;
  - TEXT_MAP lookup for 114.VIII;
  - reference payload reads (zero, single and multiple results);
  - split timings;
  - free heap and PSRAM.

## Needed to unblock (human decision)

1. **New flash baseline.** Accept the NVS difference (PHY calibration only) and adopt a new reference dump confirmed by two identical reads. Alternatively, define the flash comparison as byte-identical outside NVS. In either case, keep the `061849a3…` dump as a historical backup.
2. **Firmware fix.** Authorize the fix in the candidate:
   - accept `DEVICE_VERSION|3`, failing closed for any other value;
   - extend the read-only serial diagnostic with the A3B queries and measurements.

   The fix should be committed before the flash, so that the flashed binary has a Git checkpoint.
